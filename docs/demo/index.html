Ransom

App Review demo

A screen recording of the full unlock loop, for App Review.

Instagram is blocked by Ransom. Rex offers the deal, ten push-ups are counted by the front camera using Vision body-pose detection, and fifteen coins are paid into the bank. Nothing in this recording is staged and no video is recorded, saved or uploaded by the app itself — the on-screen preview is live and is discarded frame by frame.

What you are looking at

0:00 — the shield Ransom puts over a guarded app. “Do your reps or spend your coins.”

0:02 — the Home tab. One set is ten push-ups and pays fifteen coins. Coins are minutes.

0:04 — the set begins. The phone goes against a wall so the whole body is in shot.

0:08 — the camera counter, with the detected pose drawn over the live preview. Rex waits for stillness at the top before he starts counting.

0:10–0:19 — ten push-ups counted in real time.

0:20 — fifteen coins banked, ready to spend on the app that was blocked.
