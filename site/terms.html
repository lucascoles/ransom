What Ransom does

Ransom lets you put a price on the apps that take your time. You choose which apps to guard and which movement to pay with, and the app blocks those apps until you have completed the reps. It uses Apple's Screen Time and Family Controls to apply the block, and your device's camera or pedometer to count movement.

Never use the app while driving or anywhere that doing reps would be unsafe.

Rep counting is an estimate

The app counts reps using on-device pose detection and your phone's pedometer. Counting depends on lighting, camera position, clothing and how the movement is performed, and it will sometimes miscount in either direction. Calorie figures are rough estimates for general interest only and are not a measurement. Nothing the app reports should be relied on for medical, fitness or nutritional decisions.

Blocking is best-effort

App blocking is provided through Apple's Family Controls. It depends on iOS behaving as documented and on the permissions you grant. It can be switched off in iOS Settings at any time, and it may not block every route to every app or website. Ransom is a deliberate obstacle, not a security control, and should not be relied on as one.
