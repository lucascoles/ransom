# Ransom

An iOS screen-time blocker. You pick the apps; Ransom shields them; opening one
gets you Rex and a price in reps. Do the set, the shield lifts for the minutes
you earned. SwiftUI, iOS 17+, on Apple's Screen Time frameworks.

**Tagline:** *Earn your scroll.*

---

## Context for whoever picks this up

Every line of this app was written on a Linux container with no Swift toolchain.
It was never compiled during authoring. A GitHub Actions macOS runner
(`.github/workflows/build.yml`) is the only thing that has ever type-checked it,
and it now builds green and launches in a simulator.

That history matters: **the code compiles and runs, but almost none of it has
been exercised.** No test has ever run. Nothing has touched a real device. Treat
runtime behaviour as unverified.

## Build and run

```
open Ransom/Ransom.xcodeproj      # ⌘R to a simulator
```

`TESTING.md` covers the Apple-account realities in full. The short version:
Family Controls and App Groups are paid-account capabilities, the bundle IDs are
`com.ransom.*` and won't sign against another team, and §4 has a strip-down that
runs the whole intake flow in a simulator on a free Apple ID.

CI builds with `CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO` against a generic simulator, so a green
build says nothing about whether entitlements will provision.

## Layout

```
Ransom/
├── Ransom/                 App target
│   ├── Core/               Theme, models, AppModel store, StoreKit, notifications
│   ├── Onboarding/         16-step intake flow
│   ├── Workout/            Rep detection (Vision + CoreMotion) and the set screen
│   ├── Blocking/           Screen Time authorization and shield control
│   ├── Mascot/             Rex — eight illustrated poses, cross-faded
│   ├── Home/ Stats/ Settings/ Paywall/ Components/
├── RansomShared/           Compiled into all four targets
├── RansomShield/           Shield UI extension — the block screen
├── RansomShieldAction/     Shield button handling
├── RansomMonitor/          DeviceActivityMonitor — puts the shield back
└── Config/                 Info.plists, entitlements, StoreKit config
```

Four targets share `RansomShared/` via folder-synced groups. The app and its
extensions are separate processes that coordinate **only** through the App Group
(`group.com.ransom.app`) and Darwin notifications. `UnlockLedger` is that shared
bus; `RansomCore.Key` holds every key it uses. **If you add state the shield
needs, it goes in `UnlockLedger` and its key goes in `RansomCore.Key`** — a
missing key there was the first bug CI ever caught.

## Product decisions worth not re-litigating

**The tariff is the differentiator.** Unlock prices escalate within a day:
1× for the first two unlocks, 1.5× at the third, 2× at the fourth, 3× from the
sixth, capped at 3× and at 40 reps per set. Late-night unlocks carry a 1.5×
surcharge. Three hours away resets it. Lives in `RansomShared/Tariff.swift`;
the app and the shield both price from `UnlockLedger.currentQuote()` so they can
never quote different numbers.

**Every projection reads one curve.** `RansomPlan.reduction(onDay:)` models the
habit shrinking 35% over four weeks. Reps paid, hours saved and the plan
screen's chart all derive from it. An earlier version held unlocks flat forever
and quoted *108,000 push-ups a year* directly beneath a chart promising screen
time falls — both figures computed, mutually contradictory. Don't reintroduce a
projection that ignores the curve.

**Numbers are computed, never written.** Three reviewers independently guessed
20,000 / 15,000 / 17,000 for a figure whose real range was 12,775–98,550. Any
quantity shown to the user derives from their answers.

**Pricing:** $5.99/week or $39.99/year, annual preselected. Two products in one
subscription group, both with a 3-day trial. `Config/Ransom.storekit` lets the
paywall run locally with no App Store Connect setup.

**The intake is 16 steps** and the order is deliberate: the user names their own
apps and hours *before* the reality check, so the number is theirs, not ours.
The identity step ("I'm someone who gets stronger without going to a gym") is
echoed back on the plan screen and the paywall. The first-rep step asks for five
reps before anything is paid for, and is skippable without scolding.

**Rex is a tangerine T-rex** (`#F06027`), not the green gecko earlier commits
describe. Eight static poses in `Assets.xcassets`, cross-faded — the push-up
pose blends top and bottom frames on `depth`.

## Open, in rough priority order

- **Swift 6 landmine.** `SubscriptionManager.swift:149` captures a `var` in
  concurrent code — a warning today, a hard error under Swift 6.
- Two `AVCaptureSession` Sendable warnings in `PoseRepCounter`.
- **Green is doing the brand's work.** Rex is orange but every button, headline
  figure and progress bar is green, left over from the gecko. Reads as a green
  app with an orange mascot. A `Palette` change, not a structural one.
- `PoseRepCounter` thresholds (`requiredRange = 0.45`, `minimumConfidence = 0.3`)
  are reasoned, not measured. They need tuning against a real camera.
- `UserProfile.gender` and `GenderStep` are dead — the question was cut but the
  field and its screen remain.
- Promotional offers (the loyalty discount) need server-side signing that
  doesn't exist.
- No tests of any kind.
- Trademark clearance and an App Store name check for "Ransom".

## Conventions

- Comments explain *why*, not *what*, and earn their place. Several in this
  codebase record a bug that has already been made once — leave those.
- Debug-only hooks are wrapped in `#if DEBUG`. `-RansomStartStep <step>` opens
  the app on any intake screen with a seeded profile; that's how CI screenshots
  every screen without a UI-test target.
- Everything stays on device. No account, no server, no analytics. Pose
  detection runs through Vision locally and no video is recorded or uploaded.
